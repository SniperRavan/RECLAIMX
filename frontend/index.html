How it works

Four steps to recover your item

Simple flow, serious fraud prevention underneath.

1. Report
Describe your item with hidden verification details only you know.
2. Match
Engine scores all opposing items instantly. Score ≥ 40 → claim created.
3. Verify
Answer 3 private questions. 60% word similarity required to pass.
4. Collect
Both parties confirm handover. +10 trust points each. Case closed.
